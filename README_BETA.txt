Pokemon Stadium Overworld Models - Dramaless 2 Beta

EARLY BETA / TEST BUILD

Purpose:
- Adds Pokemon Stadium-style 3D models for overworld/follower Pokemon.
- Intended for DRAMALESS_SHAPE v2.x.
- Battle Stadium models/effects should be handled by STADIUM_BATTLE_FX.

Tested working:
- DRAMALESS_SHAPE v2.0.2
- STADIUM_BATTLE_FX v2.1.7
- Gen1Recomp modern v0.2.x test environment
- Yellow partner Pikachu in 3D
- Lead-party follower Pokemon tested: Pikachu, Rattata, Pidgey, Mankey
- Pokemon Center Chansey in 3D
- Overworld voxel rendering remains active
- StadiumBattleFX battle models/effects coexist

Known limitations:
- ROM import workflow for this overworld mod is not final.
- Static overworld Pokemon mappings are incomplete.
  Examples: Pewter Nidoran and Viridian Speary/Spearow may remain 2D.
- Non-Pikachu Yellow follower emotion bubble is not replaced yet.
- Multiple followers should be provided by compatible follower mods such as Wilds of Kanto / Followers EX; this beta does not implement its own multi-follower system.
- More maps/species/follower mods need testing.

Legal:
- No Pokemon Stadium ROM or extracted Nintendo assets are included.
- Users must provide their own legally obtained Pokemon Stadium (USA) v1.0 ROM.
- Do not redistribute ROMs or generated ROM-derived caches.

Recommended setup:
- DRAMALESS_SHAPE v2.x
- STADIUM_BATTLE_FX v2.1.7+ for battles
- This mod for overworld/followers
